Delete your Constelación account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Constelación account and associated personal data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Constelación app and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap My data and privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears. Deletion happens immediately.

On the same screen you can tap Export my data to download a copy of your data before deleting it.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and associated personal data from Constelación.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us by email. Please send the request from the email address associated with your Constelación account.

We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

Account deletion removes data associated with your account, including:

  • Profile information
  • Natal chart data
  • Chat conversations and messages
  • Saved memories and mood entries
  • Subscription and usage records stored by Constelación
  • Data cached on your device by the app

Retention

The account data listed above is deleted immediately. Technical backups may still contain it for up to 30 days, until those backups rotate automatically.

Billing records held by Google Play and our payment provider are kept for the period required by tax and accounting law, normally several years. These records do not give access to your readings, conversations or chart.

Beyond this, information is retained only where required by law, or for fraud prevention, security or other legitimate legal obligations.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your Constelación account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately through Google Play.
